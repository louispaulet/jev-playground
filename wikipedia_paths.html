How to read this

The search kept a three-path beam at each hop. When a target appeared in the current article’s links, the race stopped and returned that route.

The logs record each fetched article and the completion line, while the cached JEV rankings reveal which retained branch led to the target. The routes above are the successful branches—not every candidate explored by the beam search.
